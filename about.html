Swedish Designer in Tokyo

I'm a Web Designer based in Tokyo, with experience in UX UI design for consumer products within the financial investment industry.

I studied Informatics in the Digital Design programme at Kristianstad University in Sweden, where my interest in technology and graphic design evolved into a passion for interface design and creating digital products that enrich the lives of everyday users. Particularly interested in Ethical design discourse.

Originally from a Swedish coastal town, my frequent visits to Japan sparked a desire for a bigger and more adventurous life. In 2019 I moved to Tokyo, and ever since I've felt at home.

What I bring to a design team

Cross-functional perspective

I have experience working cross-functionally, with Product teams, Development teams, Media teams, Legal teams etc., across various projects with stakeholders in Japan, Singapore, India and European teams. Fostering collaborative relationships across various functions helps me understand how the design problem affects different departments and what parameters and constraints I am working with, to improve my design process.

End-to-end ownership

As the sole designer in my current office, I am comfortable owning the whole design project: from research and wireframes through to front-end build, localisation and post-launch optimisation.

Compliance consideration

Experience designing inside FinTech compliance constraints and balancing user needs and commercial goals within a strictly regulated environment. This often requires a level of creativity to problem solving, when navigating both technical and regulatory constraints.
